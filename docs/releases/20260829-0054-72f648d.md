# Menagerie — 20260829-0054-72f648d

_itch.io build label: `72f648d-20260829-0049`_

## New
- Character menu overhaul: a tabbed panel for stats, toggleable hidden abilities, forms/shiny variants, and per-character move and item keybinds.
- Automation bar with Auto-Hunt, Auto-Search, Auto-Mine and a new **Auto-Harvest All** button, all sharing one threat check.
- Autoplay behaviour settings: choose AoE-first (default) or single-target priority, or turn autoplay targeting off.
- Keybind legend showing platform-correct attack instructions for desktop, tablet and mobile.

## Improved
- Auto-Search and Auto-Hunt now spiral outward and keep exploring instead of stopping when nothing is nearby, in both dungeons and the overworld.
- Autoplay leads with ranged AoE, falls back to melee as enemies close in, and walks toward foes when no attack can reach.
- Floating dock buttons are drag-and-drop and remember their position, so they no longer cover exit controls.
- Stair prompts state clearly whether you are ascending or descending and let you keep auto-searching across floors.

## Fixed
- Automated actions now halt the moment an enemy is within movement plus AoE range, instead of walking you into an ambush.
- Dungeon floors no longer stop expanding at 240 tiles — floors stream outward indefinitely from a deterministic seed.

## Under the hood
- Deterministic seeded dungeon generation, plus internal deploy verification and release-note tooling.
